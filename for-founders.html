How I find winners

The Winner Loop

Most creatives stop at "does it look good". I judge every creative decision on what it does to your numbers.

  1. Research

    Customer language from reviews, comments and Reddit. What they actually say about the problem.

  2. Angles

    Lead with the problem, not the product. Sell the fix, not prevention.

  3. Hooks and scripts

    Multiple hooks per angle so tests read cleanly.

  4. Brief and build

    Tight briefs, trained editors, clean handover files.

  5. Test and judge

    A winner holds ROAS as spend grows, not one good day.

  6. Iterate

    Double down on winners, retire fatigued hooks, feed the pipeline.
